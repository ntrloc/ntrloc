For operations support

See what happened, and why — every time

Every action is logged, every override has a reason and an expiry, and support cases live on the same graph as the tickets, assets, and customers they're about.

Audit & overrides

Security posture has a history, not just a current state

Full item history

An item's history includes not just when its properties changed, but when its security posture changed — and, via the reason field, why. Policy changes are first-class events in the write layer, versioned the same way property changes are.

Temporary overrides, never permanent

A privileged user can grant specific permissions to specific users on a specific item, time-bounded by a mandatory expiry and a mandatory reason. Nothing in the override model allows a permanent, unaudited exception.

Nothing lingers unaudited

Every exception is visible, and every exception ends

Expired overrides disappear

Expired overrides cease to exist rather than sitting inactive; every creation, modification, and expiry is a write-layer event.

Default deny, always

No permission exists unless explicitly granted, and every marker on an item contributes its grants — there's no ambiguity about who can do what.

Superusers bypass policy, never audit

An emergency-access path exists for when it's needed — but every action taken through it is still fully logged.

Support cases

Tickets live on the same graph as everything else

A support case is just an item with a rich web of related tickets, assets, and customers, governed by the same markers and audit trail as everything else. The embedded BPMN engine handles status transitions and routing — deployable live, no release cycle — with ad hoc, non-linear process support (CMMN) planned alongside it for escalations that don't follow a script.
